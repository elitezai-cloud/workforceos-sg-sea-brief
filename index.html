Singapore · F&B and retail

StaffAny is the usual app for outlets you run yourself.

What their site ships

Roster, GPS + photo clock, PayrollAny.

PayrollAny shows a CPF calculator — not a proven CPF Board file. They are listed on SMEs Go Digital. That is a catalogue hit, not a grant for a given buyer.

What they do not publish

A customer login.

Case studies are Ya Kun, Foreword, Ippudo — own brands. Deputy can map client sites as “locations,” still no customer portal. We still sell F&B groups that need one trail, or that also serve clients.

SAOS owns SACE paper. TrackTik owns the tour. Pay is the hole.

SAOS

Do not lean on it yet.

Indexed copy talks SACE, client portal, facial clock. Direct fetch of saos.asia is empty. Until the site or an app listing loads, it is not a confirmed product.

Exiga = Guard Workforce

Same Singapore vendor.

TrackTik

Closest published analog.

Help centre: the customer sees only their site. GPS + no-show fill. Payroll is ADP/Xero, not CPF. Offices: US, Canada, EU, South Africa — no Asia office, no APAC data residency on their site.

Singapore · construction and office pay

Hubble is the builder’s site. Talenox is finance. Neither is this job.

Hubble.build

Main contractor OS.

Gantry / face attendance, BCA ePSS, SGBuildex CPD, work-pass at the gate. Buyer is Woh Hup / Koh Brothers class — the project owner, not a labour agency serving many principals. Hubble.Financial pays subcontractors, not worker CPF.

Talenox, JustLogin, QuickHR, Payboy

Keep them.

Native CPF/SDL/IRAS. Talenox has no native clock (Deputy / King of Time). None publish a portal for your customer. JustLogin has WhatsApp HR answers (Justina), not coverage ops.
